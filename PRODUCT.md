# PRODUCT

## Name
PineCrest

## Register
brand

## What It Is
PineCrest is a rewards platform that turns everyday social engagement into real value. Users complete simple activities (sharing, watching, referrals, daily streaks) to earn PineCoins, then redeem them.

## Primary Users
- Social media users who want lightweight rewards for engagement
- Creators and community members who can refer friends
- Students and early-career users who value micro-earnings and streak mechanics

## Brand Traits
- Trustworthy, clear, and optimistic
- Nature-inspired, modern, and slightly premium
- Confident without hype, playful only in small accents

## Tone
- Short, direct sentences
- Benefit-led, no buzzword stacking
- Prefer concrete outcomes over abstract promises

## Visual Principles
- Crisp typography, generous whitespace, soft gradients
- Motion as polish, not distraction
- Primary accent in emerald, secondary in sky/blue, with muted neutrals

## Anti-References
- Crypto-neon on black
- Generic SaaS “blue blob” hero templates
- Overly glassy UI as a default
